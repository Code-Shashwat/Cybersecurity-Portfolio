# Custom Assessment and Remediation (CAR)

## Objective
Develop a practical understanding of Qualys Custom Assessment and Remediation (CAR) and its role in endpoint assessment and remediation workflows.

## Tools & Environment
- Qualys CAR

## Key Learnings
- Overview of Qualys Custom Assessment and Remediation (CAR)  
- Understanding CAR libraries, scripts, jobs, and workflows  
- Common CAR use cases for endpoint assessment and remediation  
- Understanding CAR workflow and execution process  
- Integration and application of CAR within Policy Audit (PA)  
- Basic understanding of CAR APIs and automation capabilities  

## Key Takeaways
- CAR enables collection of custom endpoint data and supports tailored assessment and remediation workflows  
- Custom assessments improve visibility into configuration, compliance, and security gaps across endpoints
