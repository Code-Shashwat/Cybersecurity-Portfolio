# PCI Compliance

## Objective
Develop a practical understanding of Qualys PCI applications and PCI-DSS compliance workflows.

## Tools & Environment
- Qualys PCI

## Key Learnings
- Overview of PCI-DSS compliance requirements and workflows  
- Performing PCI compliance scans (internal and external)  
- Integration of PCI compliance scanning with Vulnerability Management (VM)  
- Generating and interpreting compliance reports  
- Understanding PCI web application scanning concepts  
- Working with Self-Assessment Questionnaires (SAQ)  
- Using Qualys Policy Audit for PCI compliance validation  

## Key Takeaways
- PCI-DSS compliance requires continuous monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and policy validation  
- Understanding PCI compliance workflows is essential for securing payment environments and protecting sensitive cardholder data
