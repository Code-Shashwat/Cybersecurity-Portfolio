# Policy Audit

## Objective
Develop a practical understanding of Qualys Policy Audit and compliance monitoring workflows.

## Tools & Environment
- Qualys Policy Audit (PA)

## Key Learnings
- Overview of Qualys Policy Audit and its core features  
- Understanding the Qualys Control Library and compliance controls  
- Setting up and performing compliance scans  
- Creating and managing compliance policies  
- Understanding and monitoring compliance posture  
- Using dashboards for compliance visibility and tracking  
- Working with different types of compliance reports  
- Managing policy exceptions using interactive reports  
- Using Audit Fix to identify and reduce compliance gaps  

## Key Takeaways
- Compliance management plays a critical role in maintaining enterprise security posture  
- Continuous monitoring and policy auditing help identify configuration gaps and improve system hardening
