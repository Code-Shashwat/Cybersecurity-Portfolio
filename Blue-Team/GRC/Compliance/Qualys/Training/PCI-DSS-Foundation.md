# PCI Compliance Foundation

## Objective
Develop a foundational understanding of PCI-DSS compliance requirements and payment card security best practices.

## Tools & Environment
- Qualys

## Key Learnings
- Understanding payment card vulnerabilities that can occur within merchant environments  
- Overview of PCI-DSS standards and the roles of PCI management and the PCI Security Standards Council  
- Importance of protecting cardholder and sensitive payment data  
- Understanding and preparing Self-Assessment Questionnaires (SAQ)  
- PCI-DSS security best practices and compliance requirements  

## Key Takeaways
- PCI-DSS compliance plays a critical role in securing payment systems and protecting sensitive cardholder data  
- Continuous compliance monitoring and security controls are essential for reducing payment-related risks
